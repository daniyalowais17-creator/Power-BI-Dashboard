## 📊 E-Commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard built to transform raw E-commerce transaction data into meaningful business insights having sales, profitability, customers, returns, and operations.

The project follows an end-to-end Business Intelligence workflow:

Raw Excel Data → Power Query → Data Modeling → DAX → KPI Tracking → Executive Dashboard → Business Insights
### Project Overview

Businesses often have large amounts of transactional data but lack a centralized way to monitor performance and identify areas that require attention.This project was developed to create an interactive reporting solution that allows decision-makers to quickly understand:

- Overall sales performance
- Revenue and profitability
- Category performance
- Regional performance
- Customer behavior
- Product returns
- Delivery performance
- Payment method trends
- Revenue growth over time

### Business Problem

The raw e-commerce dataset contains transactional information covering customers, products, revenue, discounts, regions, returns, delivery times, and profitability.Without a centralized reporting solution, it can be difficult to answer important business questions such as:

- How much revenue is the business generating?
- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which categories are the most profitable?
- Which regions perform best?
- How are sales changing over time?
- What is the average order value?
- What percentage of orders are returned?
- Which regions have slower delivery times?
- Are higher discounts affecting profitability?
- Which customer segments contribute most to sales?
